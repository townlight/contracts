Townlight / Townlight Contracts

Contract records that stay reviewable.

Townlight Contracts v0.2.0 ships a foundation for contract registry support, clause topic lookup, expiration tracking, renewal visibility, optional database-backed contract records, public-records export checklists, and an accessible sample UI aligned to townlight-core==1.3.0.dev2.

Shipping v0.2.0

What ships

  • Sample contract registry.
  • Clause topic lookup.
  • Expiration tracking helper.
  • Renewal visibility helper.
  • Optional registry persistence with TOWNLIGHT_CONTRACTS_REGISTRY_DB_URL.
  • Public-records export checklist.
  • Public sample UI at /townlight_contracts.

What does not ship

Townlight Contracts does not interpret legal obligations, approve renewals, execute contracts, provide legal advice, call live LLMs, use live contract management platforms, or replace a contract system of record.
